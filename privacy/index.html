Privacy policy

CallNxt is an app that clinics use to run their queue, bookings, prescriptions and WhatsApp messages. This page explains, in plain English, what happens to personal data in it, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Who is responsible for what

  • Your clinic decides. When you are a patient, the clinic you visit is the data fiduciary for your data: it decides why and how your data is used, for your care at that clinic.
  • We process it for the clinic. SUKUME TECH (a sole proprietorship of Sunil Kumar) ("we") runs CallNxt and is the clinic's data processor: we store and handle patient data only to provide the app to the clinic, on the clinic's instructions, under our data processing terms. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with other clinics.
  • For clinics and doctors themselves (your account, sign-in and billing), we are the data fiduciary.

What data is collected, and why

DataWhy
Patient name, mobile number, age and sex, and the problem described when booking To book tokens, run the queue and contact the patient about their visit
WhatsApp messages with the clinic, and reports or photos the patient sends To answer the patient and let the doctor see their reports before and during the visit
Diagnoses, prescriptions, certificates, referral letters, follow-up answers The medical record of the visit, written by the doctor
Lab reports and other files the clinic sends the patient on WhatsApp To give the patient their reports, kept in their record at the clinic
Fees, payment status and fee receipts (no card or bank details: UPI payments go straight to the clinic) For the clinic's accounts, and a receipt for the patient
Clinic and staff details, sign-in records and an audit log To run accounts securely and show who did what

Patients agree to this on WhatsApp before booking (or are told at the clinic or on the phone), and can withdraw that agreement at any time by asking for their data to be deleted (below).

Children

When the patient is a child (under 18), the parent or guardian books and agrees to this notice on the child's behalf, from their own WhatsApp number or at the clinic. The clinic uses a child's data only for the child's care, and never for tracking or advertising.

How long it is kept

  • Medical records (prescriptions, certificates, bills) are kept for at least 3 years, as Indian medical rules require.
  • When a patient asks for their data to be deleted, their name, number, profile, WhatsApp chat and the reports they sent are erased, and upcoming bookings are cancelled. Medical records are kept for the legal period but can no longer be linked to them.
  • When a clinic stops using CallNxt, it can download all its data first; we then delete it as set out in the data processing terms.
  • Encrypted backups are kept for 14 days and then overwritten.

Where it is stored, and who else sees it

  • Data is stored in India, at a data centre in India, encrypted in transit (HTTPS), with each clinic's data kept apart from every other clinic's.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) carries messages between the clinic and patients. It is a sub-processor: it receives the messages and files sent through it, under WhatsApp's own business terms.
  • Inside a clinic, only signed-in staff see patient data, and only the doctor sees clinical details.
  • A clinic in Auto mode without its own WhatsApp number uses the shared CallNxt number. Every message names the clinic, and the patient's number is used only to connect their messages to the clinic they chose; no other clinic can see them.
  • A clinic using Phone mode sends messages from its own WhatsApp, and a prescription or report goes as a link. Each link opens that one document only, can't be guessed, contains no name or number, and stops working after 30 days (or at once if the patient's data is deleted).
  • A clinic may turn on a public clinic page. It shows only the clinic's own details (name, doctor, timings, fee, address and booking number), never anything about patients. The waiting-room screen shows token numbers, not names.
  • We do not otherwise share personal data, except when the law requires it.

Your rights as a patient

  • See your data: type PRIVACY to the clinic on WhatsApp and choose "See my data". The clinic sends you a PDF of everything it holds about you.
  • Delete your data: type PRIVACY and choose "Delete my data".
  • Correct your data: tell the clinic on WhatsApp or at your visit.
  • Stop messages: send STOP on WhatsApp and the clinic sends no more reminders or updates; send START to turn them on again.
  • Complain: first to your clinic. If it isn't resolved, write to our grievance officer, Sunil Kumar, at privacy@callnxt.in. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If data leaks

If we find that personal data was exposed, we stop it, find out what happened, tell every affected clinic without delay (with what we know and what they need to do), and help them tell their patients and the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Act requires.

Changes

We will post changes here and tell clinics before they take effect.
