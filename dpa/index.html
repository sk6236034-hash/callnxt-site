Data processing terms

These terms are between each clinic using CallNxt (the data fiduciary) and SUKUME TECH (a sole proprietorship of Sunil Kumar) (the data processor), under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. They are part of the terms of use.

1. What we process, and only on your instructions

We process your patients' personal data (identity and contact details, visit and medical records, messages and files they send, payment status) only to provide CallNxt to you, as you instruct through the app. We do not use it for any other purpose, sell it, or use it to advertise.

2. Security

  • Each clinic's data is kept apart from every other clinic's by the database itself (row-level security).
  • Data travels encrypted (HTTPS); backups are encrypted; staff sign-in is protected by passwords, lockout after repeated failures, and an audit log of sensitive actions.
  • Inside your clinic, only the doctor sees clinical details; assistants see contact details and bookings.
  • Only people at SUKUME TECH (a sole proprietorship of Sunil Kumar) who need to (to run or fix the service) can reach the servers, and they are bound to keep data confidential.

3. Where data is stored

In India, at a data centre in India. We will tell you before moving it.

4. Sub-processors

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): carries messages and files between your clinic and your patients.
  • The hosting provider named in section 3.

We will tell you before adding a sub-processor, so you can object.

5. Patients' rights

We help you answer patients' requests: the app gives patients a way to ask (type PRIVACY on WhatsApp), and gives you a PDF of a patient's data and a way to erase it while keeping medical records for the legal period.

6. If data leaks

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your patients, we will tell you without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, with what happened, what data and how many patients are affected, and what we are doing about it. We will help you notify the Data Protection Board of India and your patients.

7. When the service ends

You can download all your data from Settings at any time. Within 30 days after the service ends we delete your clinic's data, except where the law requires us to keep it, and encrypted backups age out within 14 days after that.

8. Checks

On reasonable request, we will tell you how we meet these terms.
